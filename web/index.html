How it works

Four steps from screen to shelf.

01

Shape it in 3D

Pick a silhouette and handle in the live studio — the model updates as you click, not after a render queue.

02

Describe a glaze

Type what you're picturing — the studio reads it for shape, texture and colour cues and proposes four directions.

03

We hand-throw it

Your finished design goes to a small studio partner, thrown and trimmed by hand from your exact spec.

04

Fired & delivered

Glazed, kiln-fired and boxed by hand. Every piece ships a little different — that's the point.
